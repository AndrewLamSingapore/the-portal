# The Portal V2 — Evidence & Ancestry

V2 turns The Portal from a generative archive into an evidence-aware conceptual ancestry system.

## Evidence contract

Every artifact carries one of these visible states:

- **AI-CURATED** — generated discovery object; not independently verified history.
- **CONCEPTUAL-INFERENCE** — an interpretive connection or lineage, not a causal historical claim.
- **HISTORICALLY-VERIFIED** — reserved for curator-reviewed records with genuine source trails. Generation cannot assign this state.

Source URLs are never invented by generation. Verified source trails are curator-authored.

## Signature systems

- **Temporal Graph** — archive objects ordered through time with conceptual-echo edges.
- **Relationship language** — PRECEDED_BY, ECHOED_BY, FAILED_BECAUSE, BECAME_POSSIBLE_WHEN.
- **Serendipity** — generation receives recent archive context and is instructed to maximize conceptual distance rather than engagement similarity.
- **Exhibitions** — deterministic curated lenses assembled from the archive, beginning with *Futures That Arrived Decades Late* and *Machines That Tried to Replace Money*.
- **Operational guardrail** — existing production smoke verification remains the release gate.

## Public identity

The Vercel hostname remains the canonical technical endpoint until a dedicated domain is selected and purchased. Domain purchase is intentionally separate from application correctness.
