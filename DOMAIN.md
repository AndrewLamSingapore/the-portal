# Public identity

The Portal is domain-ready. The application uses relative URLs, secure headers and no Vercel-hostname dependency in its runtime logic.

A dedicated domain should be attached only after the owner selects and purchases the name. Domain purchase is a branding/financial decision and is intentionally not automated by the application release.

Recommended naming criteria: short, memorable, non-generic, defensible as a cultural archive, and not dependent on the word AI.
